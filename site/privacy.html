Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Mindlore is a journaling app. This page explains what happens to what you write, in plain terms.

No accounts, no servers

Mindlore doesn't ask you to create an account or sign in with anything. There is no Mindlore server. The app doesn't collect analytics or crash reports, and nothing about your journal is sent to us, because there's no "us" on the other end to send it to.

Where your journal lives

Your entries, recordings, and photos are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, they're also stored in your own iCloud account, the same way Apple's own apps sync, so your journal can move between your own devices. That data stays inside your iCloud account; Mindlore has no access to it and no server it passes through.

On-device AI, by default

When Mindlore uses AI, it uses Apple's on-device model by default: transcription, titles, and insights are generated on your phone, and nothing leaves it.

Bring-your-own-key OpenAI, optional

You can add your own OpenAI API key in Settings if you'd like transcription or Q&A to use OpenAI's models instead. When you do:

  • Recordings, journal text, or page photos are sent directly from your device to OpenAI, using your own key and your own OpenAI account.
  • Mindlore has no server in between and never sees this data.
  • That data is handled under OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy, not Mindlore's, since it's your account making the request.
  • Before the first request goes out, Mindlore shows a one-time screen naming OpenAI and explaining what's about to be sent. Nothing is sent without that confirmation, and you can turn AI off again at any time in Settings.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. There's nothing to sell, since Mindlore never receives it.
  • No account, so there's no profile tied to your identity beyond what your own Apple ID or OpenAI account already knows.

Your control

You can export your journal at any time from Settings (a file you keep, with your recordings and photos alongside it), delete individual entries, remove your OpenAI key, or delete the app entirely to remove everything stored locally. Turning off iCloud sync stops new data from mirroring to iCloud; existing iCloud data is managed the same way any of your iCloud data is, through your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Mindlore doesn't collect data from anyone, so there's nothing to say differently about children's data. The app isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
