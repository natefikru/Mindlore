Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback go straight to natefikru@gmail.com. Below are answers to the most common ones.

Do I need an account?
No. Open the app and start writing or recording. There's no sign-up and no login.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Recording, writing, on-device transcription, and browsing your journal all work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed if you turn on iCloud sync or add your own OpenAI key.
What does "on-device AI" require?
Titles, insights, and transcription use Apple's on-device model where your iPhone supports it (an Apple Intelligence-capable device on a recent iOS version). On a device that doesn't support it, Mindlore falls back to Apple's on-device speech recognition for transcription, and AI features like insights and Q&A show a short explanation and offer your own OpenAI key as an alternative. Capture, transcription, and browsing your journal always work, whether or not the on-device model is available.
How do I set up my own OpenAI key?
In Settings → AI, add your OpenAI API key. The first time it would actually be used, Mindlore shows a one-time screen naming OpenAI and explaining what's about to be sent, and asks you to confirm. You can remove the key or turn AI off again at any time in the same screen.
How does iCloud sync work?
Turn it on in Settings → General. Your journal syncs across your own devices through your own iCloud account, the same way Apple's own apps sync. There's no Mindlore server in between. If you're signed out of iCloud, or sync is off, the app works entirely on-device.
Can I export my journal?
Yes. Settings → General → Journal data → Export writes your entries, recordings, and photos to a folder you choose and keep. The same screen has Import, to bring a Mindlore export back in.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual entries from the journal list or the entry itself. To remove everything, delete the app, which removes everything stored on the device; if iCloud sync was on, also turn it off first in Settings so nothing new mirrors up. Your OpenAI key can be removed independently in Settings → AI.
Something's not working. What do I do?
Email natefikru@gmail.com with what happened and what device and iOS version you're on. Screenshots help.
