What it does

Record a thought or write it out. Mindlore gets you text, on your phone, using Apple's on-device model by default. As you write, it notices the names you mention, and builds a quiet map of the people, places, and projects in your life, so a question like "how's my sister been" has somewhere to look.

Private by default

There's no account to create. Your journal lives on your phone and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud account, the same way Apple Notes or Photos sync. Mindlore has no servers of its own and nothing to see.

If you'd rather use a cloud AI model for transcription or richer answers, you can add your own OpenAI key in Settings. Nothing is sent anywhere until you turn that on, and you're asked to confirm the first time it would be used. See the privacy policy for the details.
